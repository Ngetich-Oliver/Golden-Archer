Service & Safety Checklist

Thorough maintenance checks designed to improve reliability, safety and performance.

Fluids & Filters

  • Drain old engine oil and refill with manufacturer-recommended oil.
  • Replace engine oil filter, air filter and cabin filter.
  • Check and top up coolant, brake fluid, power-steering fluid and washer fluid.
  • Inspect belts, hoses and the engine bay for leaks or visible wear.

Brakes, Tyres & Chassis

  • Inspect brake pads, discs, lines and handbrake operation.
  • Check tyre tread depth, condition, pressure and wheel balance.
  • Inspect shock absorbers, springs, steering components, bushes and bearings.
  • Check wheel alignment and identify uneven tyre wear.

Electrical & Safety

  • Test battery health, alternator output and charging system.
  • Check headlights, brake lights, indicators and dashboard warnings.
  • Test seatbelts, windscreen wipers, horn and exhaust condition.
  • Scan engine, ABS, airbag, steering and transmission systems where supported.

Routine & Major Repairs

  • Scheduled mileage servicing, spark plugs and tune-ups.
  • Brake, suspension, steering and transmission repairs.
  • Engine diagnosis, repairs and complete overhauls.
  • Panel beating, frame correction, scratch removal and spray painting.

Cash-in-Lieu Insurance Settlements Accepted

Golden Archer accepts vehicle repairs funded through an insurer's cash-in-lieu settlement. The approved insurance settlement can be applied toward the repair invoice, while any agreed shortfall or top-up may be handled through an approved instalment plan.

IMPORTANT: Cash-in-lieu is an insurance settlement arrangement. It does not mean physical cash is received at the garage. All payments must use the official Till or bank accounts.
